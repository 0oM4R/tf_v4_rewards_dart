Certified Capacity Rewards Calculation

Certified Monthly Rewards Formula:

  • GB Memory: 8.0 INCA per GB
  • TB SSD: 31.5 INCA per TB
  • TB HDD: 7.0 INCA per TB
  • TB Network: 30.0 INCA per TB

Example: A node with 64GB mem, 4TB SSD, 16TB HDD, 1TB Network would earn approximately 960 INCA per month in certified rewards.

⚠️ Uptime Percentage Impact

All rewards are directly multiplied by the node's uptime percentage:

final memIncaRewards = nodeConfig.gbMem * memRewardPerGbMonth * nodeConfig.uptimePercentage / 100;

  • Minimum required uptime: 90%
  • A node with 95% uptime receives exactly 95% of potential rewards
  • Nodes with less than 90% uptime receive zero rewards
